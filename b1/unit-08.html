AUDIO_B1_U08_T01 — Managing Launch Bottlenecks & Emotional Friction Sofia: Diego, the third-party payment gateway just failed their final integration test. We cannot launch tomorrow. Diego: That is deeply frustrating, Sofia. The engineering team has been working sixty hours a week. People are near burnout. Sofia: I completely acknowledge the exhaustion. Although the delay is painful, this setback is outside our direct control. What we can control is our response. Diego: You are right. Instead of panicking, let us reframe this. Despite the immediate disappointment, discovering the glitch now prevents a catastrophic live failure. Sofia: Exactly. First, let us have the technical logs reviewed by our senior security architect. Second, let the team take tomorrow morning off to recharge. Diego: Agreed. I will also get the client account managers to explain the technical rationale to our stakeholders calmly. Sofia: Perfect. When leaders keep their composure, teams feel protected. We will weather this storm together.
