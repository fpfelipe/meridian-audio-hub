Unit 1 Pronunciation Clinic. Weak forms of have and has in connected speech. Listen to the difference between citation forms and reduced spoken forms.
🎙️ Speaker Oficina Fonética
🎙️ Áudio Guia • Dual-Anchor
Number one. I have lived here for three years. Notice the reduced contraction: I have lived becomes /aɪv lɪvd/.
🎙️ Speaker Oficina Fonética
🎙️ Áudio Guia • Dual-Anchor
Number two. She has worked in London since June. In natural connected speech, has becomes /z/: she has worked becomes /ʃiz wɜːrkt/.
🎙️ Speaker Oficina Fonética
🎙️ Áudio Guia • Dual-Anchor
Number three. How long have they studied English? The auxiliary have weakens to /həv/ or /əv/.

🎙️ Áudio Guia • Dual-Anchor
