AUDIO_B1_U01_T02 — Pronunciation Clinic: Weak Forms of Have and Has Narrator: Unit 1 Pronunciation Clinic. Weak forms of have and has in connected speech. Listen to the difference between citation forms and reduced spoken forms. Speaker: Number one. I have lived here for three years. Notice the reduced contraction: I have lived becomes /aɪv lɪvd/. Speaker: Number two. She has worked in London since June. In natural connected speech, has becomes /z/: she has worked becomes /ʃiz wɜːrkt/. Speaker: Number three. How long have they studied English? The auxiliary have weakens to /həv/ or /əv/. Speaker: Number four. We have overcome many linguistic obstacles. Listen and repeat: We've overcome many linguistic obstacles.
