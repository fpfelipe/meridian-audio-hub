Architecture of Memory: Places, Spaces & Preservation

Listen to the contracted forms of would and the intonation of condition and result. Speaker: Number one. If I had more time, I would restore this house. Listen to the contraction: If I had more time, I'd restore this house. Speaker: Number two. Notice the negative contraction wouldn't. If the budget were smaller, we wouldn't hire external architects. Speaker: Number three. I wish sentences. Listen to the stress on wish and the past verb: I wish our city had cleaner public squares. Speaker: Number four. Rising intonation on the condition clause, falling intonation on the result clause: If we protected the facade, the building would retain its soul.
