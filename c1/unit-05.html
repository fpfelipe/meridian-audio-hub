In The Structure of Scientific Revolutions, Thomas Kuhn argued that scientific progress is not an unbroken cumulative ascent, but rather a sequence of peaceful interludes punctuated by intellectually disruptive paradigm shifts. Dr. Bhatnagar, are we witnessing such a rupture in quantum information science?
D Dr. Bhatnagar Interlocutor
🌐 International English
Unquestionably, Dr. Thorne. For over seventy years, classical computation has operated within the deterministic von Neumann paradigm. However, with the demonstration of quantum error correction and topological qubits, the classical conceptual architecture is being superseded.

🇬🇧 British RP

D Dr. Bhatnagar Interlocutor
🌐 International English
While decoherence challenges cannot be dismissed lightly, historical precedents illustrate that anomalies which initially appear intractable often catalyzed the very theoretical breakthroughs that established the new paradigm.
