DR. MAYA LIN: With respect, Julian, that conflates predictive engineering with scientific comprehension. What Thomas Kuhn described as a paradigm shift is not an acceleration in calculational throughput; it is an ontological rupture. When Newtonian mechanics yielded to quantum mechanics, the very nature of physical reality was redefined. Aetheria has not redefined a single conceptual category. It has executed immense non-linear statistical regressions across billions of weights. If you ask the neural network *why* the gluon behaves as it does, it can only output probability matrices. To claim that an opaque algorithm understands quantum physics is to reduce science to functional mysticism. Without intelligible causal explanation, there is no paradigm shift—only faster mathematics. ```
